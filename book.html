TIARA NUR HADISAH,

She is a woman I met in 2026—a truly beautiful soul with the most gorgeous hair. I fondly call her CHOCOLATE, simply because her smile is as sweet as it gets.

Born on the 30th of March, 2005, She feels most at home surrounded by the deep hues of navy, rich maroon, and earthy brown—a color palette that perfectly reflects her cozy yet sophisticated presence. She has an innate ability to turn simple daily experiences into moments of calm and quiet reflection. Creativity flows effortlessly through everything she does. As someone who loves typing long paragraphs and crafting insightful articles, writing serves as her personal sanctuary where every idea finds its place. Beyond her words, swimming offers her a sense of freedom, while music provides the backdrop to her days. From the smooth R&B melodies of Miguel to the captivating rhythms of Pyrite by Frank Ocean, her musical taste reflects a deep appreciation for mood and feeling.

What makes Tiara truly special is the charming harmony between her strong ambitions and sweet, gentle side. While she dreams of cruising in a classic Mustang marked with the number 95, she finds absolute happiness in the sweetest everyday things—soft squishies, delicate flowers, and the comforting aroma of ramen. Finished off with her go-to drinks, matcha or chocolate, she reminds us that a truly beautiful life is made of bold dreams woven together with gentle, humble joys.
